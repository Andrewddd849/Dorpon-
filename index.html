What We Do

Our Core Programs

We run several impactful programs to uplift students, protect nature, and build a just society.

🎓

Student Scholarship

We identify talented but financially disadvantaged students and provide them with scholarships to continue their education without worry.

🌳

Tree Plantation Drive

Our annual tree planting campaigns bring together volunteers to green public spaces, schools, and roadsides across the local community.

📝

Scholarship Examination

Every year we organise a structured scholarship exam to fairly assess and select deserving students for financial support.

✊

Anti-Corruption Advocacy

We speak up boldly against unethical activities in our community, raising awareness and pushing for accountability and justice.

🤝

Community Outreach

Beyond our main programs, we organise awareness campaigns, workshops, and community events to create lasting social impact.

💡

Youth Empowerment

We mentor young people, helping them build skills, confidence, and a sense of civic responsibility to lead the community forward.

Annual Event

Scholarship Examination

Our annual scholarship exam is open to all eligible local students. It is designed to be fair, transparent, and accessible.

1

Registration Opens

Students register online or at our local office with required documents.

2

Written Examination

A standardised written test assessing academic aptitude and general knowledge.

3

Award Ceremony

Selected scholars receive their awards at our annual celebration event.

Our Story

Creating Real Impact

Every initiative we run is rooted in a genuine desire to create lasting positive change in our community.

🌟

Dreams Within Reach

Many students who received Dorpon scholarships have gone on to become professionals, giving back to their communities and inspiring others.

🌿

A Greener Tomorrow

Our tree planting drives have transformed bare land into green spaces, improving air quality and creating habitats for local wildlife.

⚖️

Justice & Accountability

Through peaceful advocacy, we have helped shine a light on unethical conduct, pushing for reform and holding bad actors accountable.
